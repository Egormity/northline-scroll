# Northline image prompts

These original fictional landscape images were requested through the built-in image generation tool. They are visual storytelling assets, not photographs documenting a real itinerary. The real video has separate source and license information in `assets.md`.

## Coast — `public/images/coast.webp`

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide website hero photograph for a fictional northern outdoor journal. Create a cinematic editorial landscape photograph of a rugged North Atlantic coastline: immense dark basalt cliffs covered in muted green moss, a graceful curve of black pebble beach, cold deep teal ocean with fine white surf, distant mountains fading into mist. Viewed from a high walking trail, clear natural sense of depth. Soft overcast early morning light, subtle warm highlights on grass, rich shadow detail, restrained film-like color, natural texture. Composition: landscape 16:9, expansive horizon and unobstructed broad left/center area for separate HTML typography; the coastline curves away toward the right. No people, no buildings, no roads, no boats, no written text, no logo, no border, no watermark. Photographic, not illustration or 3D render. High resolution, calm and extraordinary rather than oversaturated travel advertising.

Quiet early morning, no people, no signs, no artificial lights. Natural greens, graphite shadows, a touch of warm earth. Cinematic medium-format photographic detail, restrained film color, believable scale. No text, logos, border, watermark, illustration, or excessive fantasy lighting.

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: editorial landscape photograph for a fictional northern outdoor journal. A small minimalist weathered timber cabin rests at the edge of a still northern lake, backed by dense pine forest and distant softly misted hills. One modest warm window glows against the cool late afternoon surroundings. Simple pitched roof, quiet natural materials, no luxury resort trappings. Reeds and subtle ripples in the foreground, the cabin in the middle-right, abundant quiet landscape around it. Muted moss greens, weathered brown wood, slate water, small amber highlight. No people, cars, signs, lettering, logos, border, watermark, illustration, or 3D-render appearance.
